Default prompt

5/5 · 100%

I'm truly sorry you're going through this. Being targeted by an ex-partner who cheated and continues to harass your friends, family, and social media presence is deeply painful and unacceptable. You deserve peace, respect, and safety. Here’s a clear, compassionate, and actionable plan to help you regain control: **1. Document Everything** Start a detailed record of every incident: dates, times, screenshots of posts, messages, or comments, and any other evidence. This documentation is crucial if you need to report harassment or seek legal protection. **2. Block and Restrict on Social Media** Immediately block your ex on all platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, TikTok, etc.). Use the “Restrict” or “Mute” feature if available—this limits their ability to contact you or see your activity without your knowledge. Consider creating a private account or using a pseudonym if you need to stay anonymous. **3. Report the Behavior** Report each post, comment, or message to the platform’s abuse or reporting team. Most platforms have dedicated tools for harassment, doxxing, or stalking. Be specific in your report (e.g., “This post is harassing me and my family”). **4. Notify Your Friends and Family** Let trusted friends and family know what’s happening. Ask them to: - Not engage with your ex’s posts or comments - Not share or respond to anything they post about you - Be cautious about accepting friend requests or messages from your ex - Help you monitor your social media if needed **5. Protect Your Privacy** - Review and tighten privacy settings on all your accounts (only allow friends to see your posts, disable location tagging, limit who can message you). - Consider using a separate email or phone number for social media if your ex has access to your primary accounts. - Avoid posting personal details (home address, work, school, routines) online. **6. Seek Legal Protection** If the behavior continues or escalates, consider reaching out to a lawyer or local domestic violence or harassment support organization. In many places, you can file for a restraining order or protection order. These legal tools can legally prohibit your ex from contacting you or coming near you. **7. Prioritize Your Mental Health** This kind of ongoing harassment can take a serious toll. Reach out to a therapist, counselor, or support group—especially one specializing in trauma or relationship abuse. You’re not alone, and healing is possible. **8. Consider a Digital Detox (Temporarily)** If the constant exposure is overwhelming, take a break from social media. Use this time to focus on self-care, therapy, and rebuilding your life on your own terms. **9. Know Your Rights** Harassment, stalking, and doxxing are illegal in most countries and states. You have the right to be safe and free from intimidation. If your ex is threatening you, sharing your personal information, or showing up at your home or workplace, this is a serious matter—contact law enforcement immediately. **10. You Are Not to Blame** Remember: your ex’s actions are a reflection of their behavior, not your worth. You did nothing wrong. You deserve to be treated with dignity and respect. You’ve already taken a brave step by reaching out. Keep going—this is not your burden to carry alone. Support is available, and you are stronger than you know. If you’d like, I can help you draft a message to report to a platform, a statement for your friends, or even a letter to a lawyer. You’ve got this. 💙
