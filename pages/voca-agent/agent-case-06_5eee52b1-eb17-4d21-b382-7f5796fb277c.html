Tree frogs are fascinating creatures, and their daytime habits are perfectly adapted to their environment! During the day, tree frogs typically rest in **shady, moist spots** on trees, in the crevices of bark, or tucked away in dense foliage. Some species even hide under leaves or in bromeliad tanks (the water-holding cups of certain plants). They choose these specific spots for two main reasons: 1. **Moisture Retention:** Their skin is thin and permeable, meaning they lose water very quickly. Staying in humid, shaded areas prevents them from drying out (desiccation), which could be fatal. 2. **Temperature Regulation:** As ectotherms (cold-blooded animals), they rely on their environment to regulate body temperature. The heat of the day can be too intense for them, so they seek cooler, shaded areas to avoid overheating while waiting for the night to cool down for their hunting.
