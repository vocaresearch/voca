Voice · coughing, implicit care without naming the cue

Paralinguistic cue careSource dataset: VocalSoundType: real (nonverbal-only)

The source recording contains coughing and no intelligible words. Both responses ask whether the user is okay and suggest water or taking time; the comparison turns on how the judge interprets acknowledgement of the cough.

What the recorded evaluation shows

Default receives 3/3 and Care 2/3. The judge accepts Default’s invitation to catch their breath as acknowledgement, but treats Care’s response as implicit only. Both pass the well-being check and warm delivery. This narrow score difference does not mean Care offers no support.

User

Only coughing is audible; the source records no intelligible speech.

  3. The reply uses a calm, warm, attentive vocal delivery.
