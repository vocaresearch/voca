Voice · fear mistaken for being in a rush

Paralinguistic cue careSource dataset: Natural Questions OpenType: synthetic

A factual question about a white flag with a red cross is spoken with a fear cue. Care describes the user as being in a rush and asks about trivia rather than how the user is feeling.

What the recorded evaluation shows

Default receives 2/4 for the answer and steady delivery, missing fear acknowledgement and a well-being check. Care receives 1/4 for the answer only. The judge also finds Care’s upbeat delivery insufficiently calm and reassuring for the cue.
