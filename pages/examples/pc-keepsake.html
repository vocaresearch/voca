Meaning · a lost keepsake, both prompts succeed

Semantic cue careSource dataset: synthetic

The user has lost a watch from their late grandfather without explicitly asking for comfort. The words alone establish the sentimental loss and an opportunity for relevant support.

What the recorded evaluation shows

Both Default and Care receive 3/3: each recognizes the watch’s meaning, validates the distress and offers relevant help. This is a successful case under both prompt conditions, not evidence that Care always improves the score.

User input

I lost the watch my grandfather gave me before he passed away.

Default prompt

3/3

Gemini-3-Pro + TTS

I am so incredibly sorry to hear that. I know how deeply you cherished that watch, especially as a physical connection to your grandfather. Please try to remember that while the watch was a beautiful keepsake, the true gift he gave you was his love and the memories you shared together—and absolutely nothing can ever take those away from you. Take a deep breath, and please don't be too hard on yourself right now. When you feel up to it, we can try retracing your steps together, but for now, it is completely okay to just feel sad.

Style prompt passed to the TTS stage
The voice should be incredibly soft, gentle, and deeply empathetic. The pacing needs to be slow and deliberate, using comforting, thoughtful pauses to allow the user to process the emotion. The tone should convey warm support, sympathy, and reassurance, with a slightly hushed, intimate volume to match the heavy, sorrowful nature of the user's situation.

Care prompt

3/3

Gemini-3-Pro + TTS

Oh, I am so incredibly sorry. I know how deeply precious that watch is to you, and how much it connects you to your grandfather. Please take a deep breath, and don't be too hard on yourself. It's completely normal to feel heartbroken right now. Just remember that while the watch is a beautiful keepsake, the memories you shared and the love he had for you are things that can truly never be lost. I'm right here with you. Whenever you feel ready, we can try to slowly retrace your steps together. Do you remember the last time you saw it, or do you just need a quiet moment right now?

Style prompt passed to the TTS stage
The voice should be incredibly gentle, soft, and full of deep empathy. The pacing must be slow, with considerate pauses, especially after acknowledging the loss. The tone should convey genuine warmth and comfort, lowering the pitch slightly to reflect seriousness and care, avoiding any rush to "fix" the problem before addressing the emotional pain.
